the week binance lost $6b and ethereum charged $0.93 for it

seven morning pulls, 7,900 tweets, and the week splits clean down thursday. the first half was still last weekend's material: strive buying 6,182 btc at an average $82,732, bitmine stacking eth to 4.95% of supply, and us energy execs resigning in disgust over a $25b funnel the trump family was quietly expediting. then 10-29 landed — binance drained for ~$5.7b across ethereum, bitcoin and tron, withdrawals paused, aave freezing v3 and v4, cz answering "loss might be a bit higher than that, but will cover" at 6.1k likes. by friday coindesk had the mechanism (eip-7702, ~$2.5b out of a single wallet in four minutes) and the ethereum crowd had already moved on to the gas receipt and to flights. the quiet spine ran five mornings underneath all of it: devcon mumbai, starting as ticket-and-coffee bundles on saturday and ending the next weekend as an argument about whether india was the right place for it. iran ran three straight mornings and then stopped existing.

Sat · Sun · Mon · Thu · Fri · Sat · Sun
