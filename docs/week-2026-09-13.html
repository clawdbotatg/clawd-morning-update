a resignation letter on wednesday, a threat report on friday, same company

the week opened with gpt-6 astra doing party tricks — banteg handed it a robot, a paintbrush and a camera and it painted the golden gate bridge in real life — and ended with tibo apologizing for skills that fire too often and subscriptions paused to protect existing users. the middle belonged to anthropic, twice: jacob coxon's resignation letter cleared 86k retweets on wednesday warning the company is racing toward superintelligence, then on friday anthropic published its own threat report naming deepseek and moonshot for relaying customer prompts to claude through fraudulent accounts, and by saturday zmanian was passing along rumors that sixteen people at kimi had been taken in. underneath the lab noise ethereum just kept building at the same pace it always does — qivalis' 37 european banks on tuesday, the privacy roadmap testnet going public on glamsterdam sunday — while the loudest ethereum post of the week was four words with no numbers in it. the degen half of the timeline moved to two new chains: solana shipped world's prediction market to a million waitlisted users on wednesday, and robinhood chain spent thursday through sunday listing $2m memecoins and inventing tokenized equity. the trump $5,000 dividend ran all seven days and the top post about it was someone explaining why it won't arrive.
